# THE WORLD TODAY — Monday 23 November 2026

A front-page dense summary of 23 November 2026's world news in one image: 31 stories across 6 regions.

**Heroes:** AI rout hits Asia (Kospi −3.3% to 4,708.54) · Guterres: regulate AI before it's too late (Bridgetown, 150 delegates) · US lead over China at record low (Asia Power Index 2026: US 78.9, China 73.6, gap 5.3).

**Also inside:** 10 more stories, a world map with numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK box that keeps out stories from other days.

## How it works

- `draw.py` renders `entry.png` (1024×1024) with PIL.
- `main.py` (stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
